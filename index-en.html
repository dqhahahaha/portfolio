Architecture

An AI multi-agent working system

From data capture to final delivery it isn't one model doing the work — it is 9 specialised agents, each with an explicit input/output contract, layered into an auditable automation pipeline.

Why a system, not a tool

A GPT wrapper is not enough — medical intelligence needs multi-source integration, structured processing and layered auditing. Each agent owns an independent input/output contract and they communicate through normalised data formats, so an error can be traced back to the exact stage that produced it.

Collector · data capture Analyst · threat grading Architect · visual design Coder · implementation Reporter · documents QA · auditing Harness eng. · governance

What matters is not having 9 agents — it is that every stage produces a verifiable output. The QA layer's exhaustive PMID verification, exact impact-factor matching and multi-round gate audits are what make the final deliverable trustworthy.

Methodology

Two reusable methods for medical intelligence

One answers "where is the evidence, and did we search exhaustively?"; the other answers "which assets exist in this area, who is advancing them, and how is competition moving?" Both keep provenance, judgement and uncertainty inside the output, so results can be re-checked and continuously updated.

SYSTEM 01

Multi-database literature retrieval

An auditable retrieval workflow for clinical research questions. Sensitivity is set first by question and purpose, then a database-specific query is written for each source — never one string mechanically applied everywhere.

1Structure the question
Define disease/population, intervention or exposure, retrieval mode, cut-off date and inclusion/exclusion criteria.
2Query each source specifically
PubMed, Europe PMC and ClinicalTrials.gov as the core; Crossref to recover identifiers, OpenAlex added conditionally.
3Standardise and de-duplicate
Keep every source's raw record; merge views on PMID, DOI, registration number and explainable candidate links.
4Screen and hand off
Record include/exclude/pending per row with reasons; hand off to evidence extraction only once retrieval is complete.

Key quality gates

Declared database coveragePagination & truncation checksQuery / date / hash Exclusion reasonsTraceable identifiersTrials kept separate from papers

SYSTEM 02

Disease-area landscape retrieval

Builds the in-development drug landscape for a disease area. The point is not how many trials were scraped, but resolving trials, product identities, rights holders and indication programmes into competitive intelligence you can compare and drill into.

1Broad search, iteratively expanded
Start from disease, target, MOA and modality; expand with development codes, historical aliases and partnering events.
2Record preservation and trial parsing
Store immutable raw snapshots first; records lacking a registration ID, with low confidence or ambiguous matches must never disappear silently.
3Five-layer product identity
Separate active moiety, R&D asset, formulation/configuration, indication programme and trial; combination regimens are modelled independently.
4Evidence tracing and landscape generation
Trace sponsor and asset rights holder separately; unresolved identities go to human review, and competitive metrics are computed only after gates pass.

Key quality gates

Zero silent loss of raw recordsDual counting: molecules vs assetsSponsor ≠ rights holder Auto-merge only on strong evidenceSuspected matches to human reviewFailures marked partial

A shared foundation: build the evidence chain before drawing conclusions

TraceableEvery conclusion leads back to its source, original wording and retrieval context.
AuditableQueries, run timestamps, versions and screening/merge rationales are all retained.
Reversible by designDe-duplication is not deletion; raw records survive, and undo/split are production gates.
BoundedDatabase coverage, access limits, pending review items and accountability for medical judgement are all stated.
